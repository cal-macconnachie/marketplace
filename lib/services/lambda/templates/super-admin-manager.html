⏱️ One-Time Purchase Duration

For non-recurring products, you can set a custom access duration by adding a metadata field with key "time" and value in seconds.

Examples:
• time: 1800 = 30 minutes
• time: 3600 = 1 hour (default)
• time: 86400 = 24 hours
• time: 604800 = 7 days
• time: 31536000 = 1 year
• time: 3155673600 = 100 years ("forever")
